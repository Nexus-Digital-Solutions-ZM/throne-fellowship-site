A fellowship built on family, faith, and belonging

Throne of Families and Nations Fellowship exists to gather households from every background into one body of worship, prayer, and mutual care. We believe the local church is where nations begin to heal — one family at a time.

Under the leadership of our pastoral team, we hold weekly services, prayer meetings, and community gatherings rooted in Scripture, hospitality, and honest fellowship. [Replace with the Fellowship's own vision & leadership write-up.]
