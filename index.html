A second family for every nation

Founded in 2024 by Chileshe Simbule, Throne of Families and Nations is a fellowship that meets three evenings a week — bringing together families from different backgrounds and different nations to worship, be taught the Word, be healed, and hear the voice of God.

We are a home for anyone who needs to be built, equipped, healed, and restored — a second family, wherever in the world you're joining from.
