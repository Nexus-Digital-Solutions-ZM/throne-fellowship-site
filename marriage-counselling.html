Marriage & Counselling

Rebuilding broken marriages, and equipping families to stand strong — through the Word, prayer, and pastoral care.

A ministry devoted to the family

Part of the heart of Throne of Families and Nations is reaching families through the counselling of marriages and the rebuilding of broken marriages. We believe restored homes are the foundation of restored nations.

Whether your marriage needs strengthening, healing, or a fresh start, our counselling ministry offers a confidential, faith-centred space to be heard, prayed for, and guided through Scripture.

What our counselling ministry offers

Pre-Marital Guidance

Preparing couples for marriage with biblical foundations, communication tools, and prayer.

Marriage Restoration

Walking alongside couples through conflict, brokenness, and the process of rebuilding trust.

Family & Parenting

Support for the wider family unit — parenting, extended-family conflict, and household unity.

All sessions are confidential and handled with care by our pastoral team. Reach out via our Prayer Line to request a counselling session.
