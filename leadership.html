Pastoral Leadership

The Throne of Families and Nations was founded according to the prophetic, and is led with a heart for families, healing, and the nations.

Founders

Chileshe Simbule

Founder

Founded Throne of Families and Nations in 2024. Ministers in healing, the prophetic, and the teaching of God's Word to families across nations.

Also leads Chileshe Simbule Lifestyle TV, sharing teaching and testimony beyond the fellowship's own gatherings.

Grace Simbule

Co-Founder

Co-founder of the fellowship, ministering alongside Chileshe Simbule in worship, prayer, and pastoral care for the families of the fellowship.

The wider team

Alongside our founders stands a pastoral team of ministers serving in healing, counselling, and family outreach — equipping believers for ministry and hosting other ministers to help reach as many families as possible.

In submission to Rivers of Joy Church
