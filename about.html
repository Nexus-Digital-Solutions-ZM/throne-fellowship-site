Our Story

How a fellowship that started in 2024 became a second home for families across the nations.

Founded in 2024, for every family

Throne of Families and Nations is a fellowship that meets three days a week — Monday, Wednesday, and Friday, from 21:00 to 22:30 — bringing together people from different backgrounds and different nations to worship God together.

We are a ministry reaching out to different nations and different families through healing, through the prophetic, through the teaching of God's Word, and through the counselling of marriages and the rebuilding of broken homes.

Throne of Families and Nations is a second home for everyone who needs to be taught, to be built, to be equipped for ministry, to be healed, and to hear what God has to say to them.

One family, many nations

The Throne of Families and Nations is a second family that involves people from different locations around the world — joining us from Europe (Belgium), from Canada, from South Africa, from Saudi Arabia, from Zambia, and many more nations besides. It is the different nations and different families coming before the face of God, together.

A ministry of healing, prophecy, and family restoration

Our reach is expressed through healing, through the prophetic word, through the teaching of Scripture, and through the counselling and rebuilding of marriages and families. We host and equip other ministers, and we welcome partners who want to help us reach as many families as possible.
